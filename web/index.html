      The Unified Groove.

      🎯

      One Platter

      No split screens. Deck A protrudes outward from the ring, Deck B inward. Angle is time, so one revolution is one deck cycle and the playhead’s position on the circle is the playback position.

      🎼

      Harmonic Engine

      Key is measured from the audio with a chromagram correlated against the Krumhansl-Kessler profiles, then placed on the Camelot wheel — so compatibility scores describe the actual recording.

      ⚡

      Auto Beat Sync

      Tempo and beat phase are measured from a spectral-flux onset envelope. A track whose tempo cannot be determined is skipped rather than warped by a guess.

      Your Screen is the Canvas.

      Gestures are global — they apply anywhere on the screen, and two can run at once. A gesture ends the moment its own conditions stop being met, so you can run one straight into another.

      🖐️

      3 Fingers

      Move, zoom and rotate the whole platter, to work precisely on part of a track

      👆

      1 Finger
